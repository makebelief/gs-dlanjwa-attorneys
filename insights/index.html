CLIENT GUIDE / 01

Understanding a civil dispute

What to prepare before consulting an attorney about a contract, property or other civil disagreement.

  • Prepare copies of contracts, messages, payment records and any court papers.
  • Write down the timeline, identify the parties and note any deadlines.
  • Discuss remedies and the appropriate court or dispute-resolution route with a qualified attorney.

Every matter is different. Please obtain advice that addresses your specific circumstances.

CLIENT GUIDE / 02

Facing a workplace dispute?

Key documents and dates that may be relevant when raising a labour or employment-law question.

  • Keep employment contracts, payslips, disciplinary letters and written correspondence.
  • Note the date of the incident or dismissal because dispute processes can have strict time limits.
  • Explain whether you represent an employer or an employee and what outcome you seek.

Every matter is different. Please obtain advice that addresses your specific circumstances.

CLIENT GUIDE / 03

Planning for a family-law consultation

How to organise the essential information before seeking advice about divorce, maintenance or care arrangements.

  • Gather marriage documents, maintenance or parenting orders and any relevant communication.
  • List your questions and immediate concerns, particularly those involving children.
  • Discuss privacy, costs and available procedures with your attorney.

Every matter is different. Please obtain advice that addresses your specific circumstances.

CLIENT GUIDE / 04

Considering a Road Accident Fund claim

Why early record keeping, treatment documents and claim deadlines can matter.

  • Preserve accident reports, medical documents, identification and proof of income where relevant.
  • Record important dates and any interactions with insurers or public bodies.
  • Ask for advice promptly because RAF claims may involve strict requirements and time limits.

Every matter is different. Please obtain advice that addresses your specific circumstances.
